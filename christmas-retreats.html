Common Questions

Christmas Bookings — FAQ

Is Shekhinah available for Christmas events in December 2026?
Yes — we accept bookings for Christmas conventions, youth camps and New Year vigils throughout December. However, December is our busiest month and dates fill quickly. WhatsApp or call +91 70938 57222 right away to check availability for your specific dates.
What types of Christmas events do you host?
We host Christmas conventions (Dec 20–27), youth Christmas camps (school holiday window), and New Year's Eve watch-night vigils (Dec 31 – Jan 1). All events have access to the full campus — auditorium, accommodation and in-house dining.
What is the weather like in December near Hyderabad?
December is one of the most pleasant months at our campus — temperatures range from 18–28°C with clear skies. Ideal for indoor worship sessions in the A/C auditorium and outdoor activities on the sports fields and amphitheatre.
Can you provide Christmas catering and special menus for Christmas Day?
Yes. Our experienced kitchen team can prepare customised Christmas menus including a special Christmas Day celebration meal and festive options throughout your event. In-house catering is available for up to 350 guests simultaneously.
How far in advance should we book for Christmas?
As early as possible. Churches across Telangana and Andhra Pradesh book December dates in October and November. The sooner you enquire, the more likely your preferred dates will still be available.
How far is Shekhinah from Hyderabad?
Shekhinah is approximately 1 hour from Hyderabad city centre, located on NH-44 at the Shekhinah Campus, Ramanthapur. Easy road access from Hyderabad, Secunderabad, Bengaluru and Chennai.
